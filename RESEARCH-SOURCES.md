## Conflicts disclosed rather than hidden

### Opening hours

- Current official Instagram: 11:00 AM–12:30 AM, seven days a week.
- Live Google listing: 9:30 AM–12:30 AM daily.
- Tripadvisor and an archived official page: 11:00 AM–11:00 PM.
- Zomato delivery: service begins around 11:30 AM.

The public website uses the current first-party Instagram hours and tells guests that Google shows an earlier opening. Visitors are asked to call for the day’s schedule.

## Cuisine and current public menu

Zayit’s current Instagram describes “Indian and Mediterranean” food. The current Zomato menu lists North Indian, Chinese, biryani, pizza, sandwiches, pasta, desserts and beverages.

The website includes selected, currently visible menu items without inventing prices:

- Tandoor and starters: Paneer Tikka, Paneer Malai Kebab, Hara Bhara Kebab, Dahi ke Kebab, Lahsuni Paneer Tikka, Chicken Tikka, Chicken Malai Kebab, Lahsuni Chicken Tikka and Chicken 65.
- Vegetarian mains: Dal Makhani, Paneer Butter Masala, Paneer Lababdar, Kadai Paneer, Palak Paneer, Methi Malai Corn, Malai Kofta and Yellow Dal Tadka.
- Non-vegetarian mains: Butter Chicken, Chicken Lahori, Chicken Curry, Butter Garlic Chicken, Kadhai Chicken, Chicken Korma, Chicken Changezi, Chicken Rara, Mutton Rara, Mutton Korma, Mutton Rogan Josh and Bhuna Mutton.
- Breads and rice: Tandoori Roti, Butter Roti, Missi Roti, Naan, Garlic Naan, Laccha Paratha, Bajre ki Roti, Cheese Naan, Jeera Rice, Dum Chicken Biryani, Dum Veg Biryani, Chicken Mandi and Mutton Biryani.
- Café and drinks: sandwiches, Kathi rolls, shakes, non-alcoholic Piña Colada, Blue Lagoon, lemon soda, cappuccino, americano and cold coffee.

Prices are intentionally omitted because the live platform is the reliable changing source.

## Guest favourites, ratings and reviews

No public POS or verified sales data was found, so the website does not claim “best sellers.” It uses the label “most mentioned by public guests” for dishes that recur in reviews:

- Laal Maas
- Chicken Lahori
- Dal Makhani with Butter Roti
- Butter Chicken
- Chicken Tikka and kebabs

Some public reviews mention Galaouti Kebab, Rumali Roti, Lagan Dum Murgh and Chicken Shawarma, but these do not appear on the current delivery menu. They were not presented as current menu guarantees.

Rating snapshots used on the site:

- Google: 4.8 from 492 reviews on the check date, displayed durably as 490+.
- Tripadvisor: 5.0 from 9 traveller reviews.
- Zomato: 4.2 delivery rating. Cached/rendered variants differed slightly, so no review count is shown.

Public-review themes were paraphrased instead of copying review text:

- Fort-facing terrace and evening atmosphere
- Friendly, considerate hosting
- Broad choice and generous portions
- Authentic flavour and perceived value

There are also minority reports of slow service, availability issues and inconsistent coordination. The site avoids absolute claims such as “universally loved.”

## Chef information

No current chef identity, title, biography or official portrait could be verified from a first-party source. One user review names “Chef Safa,” but that is not adequate evidence of the current role. The site therefore includes an explicit owner-content placeholder.
