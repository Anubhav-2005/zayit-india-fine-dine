# Owner content checklist before public launch

The current deployment should remain a private working preview until the items below are confirmed.

## Required: original photography

Replace the private-preview social crops in `public/images/` with the owner’s original, licensed high-resolution exports. Keep the same filenames to preserve the design:

- `zayit-01.jpg` through `zayit-06.jpg`
- `zayit-ambience-dining.jpg`
- `zayit-ambience-spaces.jpg`
- `zayit-kitchen-fire.jpg`

Recommended delivery:

- Original JPEG or WebP, minimum 2000 px on the long edge
- No screenshots, social-media UI, watermarks or downloaded traveller photos
- Include photographer credit and written web-use permission where the photographer is not the owner
- Identify the actual dish or room in each image so captions can be specific and accurate

The licensed Jaisalmer hero may remain if the CC BY-SA credit is retained.

## Required: chef profile

Replace the marked chef placeholder with:

- Current chef’s full name and exact title
- Owner-approved biography of 80–120 words
- Original portrait, minimum 2000 × 2500 px
- Date the role was confirmed
- Any culinary claims, awards or previous restaurants with supporting links

Do not use “Chef Safa” based only on a historic customer review.

## Required: business confirmation

- Confirm whether public opening hours should be 11:00 AM–12:30 AM or Google’s 9:30 AM–12:30 AM.
- Update the Google Business profile if its hours are wrong.
- Confirm `zayitindia@gmail.com` is monitored. Remove it from the site if not.
- Confirm whether `+91 70231 45500` remains an active secondary number.
- Confirm whether the restaurant accepts WhatsApp reservations. The site currently uses telephone only.
- Provide the canonical official Facebook page URL, or confirm that no official page exists.
- Repair or redirect `zayitindia.com`, which currently shows a hosting-provider default page.
- Choose and authorize a real enquiry-delivery service before public launch. The current accessible forms validate details and open the visitor’s email application; they do not silently transmit or confirm anything.
- Supply reservation confirmation, cancellation and privacy wording before enabling a true booking workflow.

## Required: menu and commercial claims

- Export the current dine-in menu with prices as a PDF or structured list.
- Identify temporary, seasonal and delivery-only items.
- Provide POS-backed top-selling dishes if the website should use “best sellers.”
- Confirm whether Laal Maas is currently available.
- Confirm whether Galaouti Kebab, Rumali Roti, Lagan Dum Murgh and Chicken Shawarma are current dine-in items.
- Confirm service charges, taxes and any reservation/deposit policy before publishing them.

## Required: guest and venue details

Confirm before adding claims about:

- Vegan, Jain, gluten-free, nut-free or halal preparation
- Allergen-separation procedures
- Alcohol service
- Step-free access or elevator availability to the first floor
- Valet, street or dedicated parking
- Private dining and group capacity
- Child seats, pets, smoking areas and dress code
- Takeaway and delivery zones
- Accepted card and digital-payment methods
- Event programming, dates, ticketing and entertainment
- Private dining availability, space exclusivity, capacity, minimum spend, deposits and AV facilities

## Optional but valuable

- Owner/founder story and approved portrait
- Architectural/interior-design story and designer credit
- Signature table, terrace and sunset-view details
- Current awards and press links
- Official reservation URL
- Google review deep link for post-visit follow-up
- Preferred Instagram post URLs for embeds, if embeds are desired
